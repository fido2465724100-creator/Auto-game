Rules for working on this project:

1. Work incrementally and do not jump ahead.
2. Always explain what module you are implementing and why.
3. Keep business logic separate from UI.
4. Prefer data-driven structures over hardcoded values.
5. Use strong TypeScript types.
6. When proposing formulas, keep them simple and tunable.
7. When creating files, prefer complete minimal working files.
8. When something is not yet implemented, leave a clear TODO.
9. Avoid fake completeness. It is better to implement a smaller real slice than a large pseudo-solution.
10. Keep future Google Cloud deployment in mind, but do not overengineer infra in MVP.
